Frequently Asked Questions

You can track your order at any time by visiting My Orders and clicking "Track Shipment". Alternatively, you can open our AI Chatbot and simply type "Where is my order?" or "What is the status of ORD1001?" for instant real-time checkpoints.

Orders in PLACED and CONFIRMED states can be freely cancelled from your order details page or via the AI Chatbot. Once an order reaches PACKED, cancellation is restricted; after SHIPPED, cancellation is not possible.

ShopSphere offers a 7-day hassle-free return guarantee on delivered items. Eligible reasons include damaged product, wrong item, or size issues. Once approved, mock refunds are issued back to the payment method within simulated banking guidelines.

Our AI Assistant uses an agentic architecture: it plans structured tool proposals across 23 tools. Every tool call is intercepted by an external policy engine (AegisAI) before any database action is performed. Enable Dev Mode in the chat window to view the security interception metadata!
